Questions

Dashboards, answered.

What data sources can you connect?

Most business tools — CRMs, accounting software, scheduling platforms, spreadsheets, and industry-specific systems. If your data lives somewhere with a way to access it, we can typically pull it into your dashboard.

Do I need a data or IT team to maintain it?

No. We build dashboards to run on their own once they're live, and we stay on to handle updates and adjustments as your business or data sources change.

Can different people see different views?

Yes. We can build role-based views so an owner, a manager, and a frontline team member each see the information that's relevant to them.

How long does a dashboard build take?

It depends on how many systems need to be connected and how complex the reporting logic is. After your onboarding form, we'll scope your specific project and give you a clear timeline.
