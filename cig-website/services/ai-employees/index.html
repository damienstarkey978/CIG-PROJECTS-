Questions

AI employees, answered.

Will an AI employee replace my staff?

Most of our clients use AI employees to absorb the repetitive, high-volume work — freeing their existing team to focus on higher-value conversations and decisions, rather than replacing people outright.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

We build clear escalation rules into every AI employee. When a request falls outside what it's equipped to handle, it hands off to a human on your team instead of guessing.

Is my data safe?

Your AI employee only accesses the systems and data you connect it to, scoped specifically to its job. We'll walk through data handling and access as part of scoping your project.

How is this different from a chatbot plugin?

Most chatbot plugins are limited to answering questions from a script. An AI employee is built to actually complete tasks — qualifying a lead, processing a document, updating a record — inside your real systems.
