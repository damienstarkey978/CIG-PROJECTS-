ON GO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Contact: support@ongo-app.com

ON GO (“we”, “us”) helps people discover live food and drink offers near the Jacksonville Beaches and earn visit points at participating venues. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. The live product is at https://ongo-app.com and may also be distributed as a native wrapper through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Points are not cash. Visit points and rewards are loyalty incentives from participating venues. They are not money, bank deposits, or guaranteed cash payouts.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — name, email, password (stored hashed), account type (customer or business), and profile details you provide.
  • Location — precise location when you allow it, used to show nearby venues and to verify you are at a venue when earning visit points. We do not need background location for the beta product.
  • Camera — used only if you scan a venue QR code in the app. We do not continuously record or upload your camera feed.
  • Usage & device — basic app usage, device type, and diagnostics needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Business content — venue profiles, offers, QR tokens, and campaign settings submitted by business accounts.

2. How we use information

  • Operate Explore, Rewards, and Business features.
  • Authenticate accounts and prevent abuse (for example, duplicate visit-point awards).
  • Verify qualifying visits with location + QR eligibility on our servers.
  • Show points balances, activity history, and reward redemptions.
  • Communicate about your account, security, or material product changes.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not treat visit points as cash or transfer them to a bank account.
  • We do not require payment-card billing for the consumer beta; any future business subscription billing (for example Stripe) is optional and separate from visit points.

4. Sharing

We share data with service providers that help us host and operate ON GO (for example hosting, databases, email delivery) under contractual limits. Participating venues see activity related to their own offers and redemptions (views, qualifying visits, rewards fulfilled) — not your password or unrelated account secrets. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

5. Retention

We keep account, points-ledger, and visit/redemption records as long as needed to provide the product, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request account deletion by contacting support; some records may be retained in anonymized or audit form.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and server-side checks for point awards. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a unique password.

7. Children

ON GO is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Age ratings for store listings are chosen to reflect food & drink venue discovery (see App Store / Play Console questionnaires).

8. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location and camera permissions in system settings (some features like visit points will not work without location).
  • Browse without signing in; an account is required to earn or redeem points.
  • Email support@ongo-app.com for access, correction, or deletion requests.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
