Wine Babe Scanner support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Send us a note below. Add your email if you'd like a reply.

Common questions

How many free scans do I get? Five scans without an account. After that, a free account (name and email) keeps you scanning.

How do I sign in on a new phone? Tap Sign in at the top of the app and enter your email. We'll email you a 6 digit code.

How do I delete my account? In the app, tap Account at the top, then Delete my account.
