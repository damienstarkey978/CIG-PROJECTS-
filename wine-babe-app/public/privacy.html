Privacy Policy

Wine Babe Scanner · Last updated November 18, 2026

Wine Babe Scanner lets you scan a wine label and see its sugar, calories and how open the winery is about what's in the bottle. This page explains what the app collects and why. We keep it to what the app needs to work.

What we collect

  • Your account, if you make one: your name, email address and, only if you add it, your phone number. We use these to run your account and sign you in. Accounts are free.
  • Label photos and searches: the photo or wine name you scan is sent to our server to identify the wine. Photos are used only for that scan and are deleted when it finishes. The wine details we find (not your photo) are kept so the next person who scans that wine gets an answer faster.
  • Scan counts: to give everyone a few free scans and keep costs in check, we count scans per device (using a random ID stored in a cookie), per account and per internet address. Internet addresses are stored only as a one way scrambled code, never the address itself.
  • Feedback you send: the message, the email you add (optional), and basic app details such as the app version and type of device, so we can fix bugs.
  • Recent scans: your list of recent scans is saved on your own device, not on our server.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't track your location.

Services we use

We rely on a few providers to run the app, and they handle data only to provide their service to us:

  • Netlify hosts the app and stores account, scan count and feedback records.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that reads labels and looks up wine information. Label photos and wine names are sent to it for each scan.
  • Resend sends emails such as sign in codes.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: tap Account at the top, then Delete my account. This removes your name, email and phone number and your account's scan counts. Feedback you sent stays, with your name and email removed.

Age

Wine Babe Scanner is about alcoholic drinks and is meant for adults of legal drinking age. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions or requests? Use Share feedback in the app or the form on our support page.
